questions

go on then.

can anyone see which troll is mine?

no. the art is public. who holds it is an encrypted record only you can read. every move is checked with a zero-knowledge proof and batched with everyone else's.

can they trace my line back to me?

no. your line goes in through the relay with a proof that you hold a troll. not which troll, not which wallet.

can i change my line?

yes. as many times as you've got relay credits. you can also switch it off, so the troll sells with a clean mouth.

can a line be deleted?

not by us. it's on bitcoin. the trollbox only shows the latest one per troll, so a new line replaces the old one on screen.

is it in my wallet?

no. it lives in your vault on ord dropz. same as shielded nakas. a wallet-held version is on ob1's roadmap.

who made this?

strangersolemn. protocol-specific art on bitcoin: the ord lot, block party, block clocks. this one's for ob1's shielded protocol.
